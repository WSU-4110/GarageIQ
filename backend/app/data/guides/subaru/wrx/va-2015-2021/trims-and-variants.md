# WRX versus WRX STI

The VA generation covers both the turbocharged 2.0 L WRX and the WRX STI. They are tuned and modded differently, so confirm which one you own before following any guide. A tune, turbo or exhaust part for the WRX will not necessarily fit an STI.

# STI changes during the run

Subaru released several limited STI editions across the generation, such as the 2015 Launch Edition and the 2016 Hyper Blue. For the 2018 model year the STI Type RA was introduced as a more track-focused variant, and upgraded pistons raised STI output by 5 horsepower to 310, carried onto all 2019 STI models. Model year matters when choosing internals or a tune.

# Special editions

Limited editions such as Series.Gray and Series.White were mostly paint, wheel and trim packages, with some Series.Gray cars also getting upgraded suspension and brakes. Check your car's actual equipment rather than assuming it matches a base trim.
