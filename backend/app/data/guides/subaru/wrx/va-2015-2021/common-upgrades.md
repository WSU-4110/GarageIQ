# Power

The turbocharged WRX responds to a tune, intake and exhaust. Choose a reputable tuner and keep oil changes frequent. An upgraded intercooler helps with heat on hard runs.

# Handling and drivetrain

Sway bars, springs or coilovers and a performance alignment improve handling. Clutch and differential choices depend on how much power you plan to add. The STI is a different car from the WRX, so confirm which you own.
