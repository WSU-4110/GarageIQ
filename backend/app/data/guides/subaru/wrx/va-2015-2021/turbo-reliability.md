# Before adding boost

Keep up with oil changes using quality oil and watch for knock. Turbocharged boxer engines are sensitive to fueling and heat, so tuning should be done by a reputable tuner.

# Cooling and heat

Stock intercooler capacity can limit repeated hard runs. Consider heat management before aggressive power goals.
