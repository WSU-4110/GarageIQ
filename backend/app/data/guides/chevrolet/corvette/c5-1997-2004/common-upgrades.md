# Bolt-on power

The LS1 responds well to long-tube headers, a high-flow cat or cat delete where legal, a cold air intake and a matching PCM tune. Gains are modest without a tune, so budget for one. A camshaft upgrade is the usual next step and needs a tune and, on automatics, a converter that suits it.

# Handling and brakes

Tires and alignment give the biggest improvement per dollar. Upgrade brake fluid and pads before big brake kits for street use. If the car has selective ride control, check that coilover or shock choices work with it.

# Reliability

Common maintenance items include the optispark distributor, water pump and cooling hoses, and the electronic parts around the steering column. Inspect these before adding power.
