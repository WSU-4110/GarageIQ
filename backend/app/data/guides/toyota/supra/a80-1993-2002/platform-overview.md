# Platform

The fourth-generation Supra (A80) was assembled at the Motomachi plant and sold in the US until 1998, with worldwide production ending in 2002. It uses an inline-six layout, like earlier Supras.

# Engines and transmissions

The naturally aspirated 2JZ-GE and the sequential twin-turbo 2JZ-GTE are the two engines. Turbo cars were offered with a 6-speed manual or a 4-speed automatic. Confirm which engine your car has, since turbo parts apply only to the 2JZ-GTE. Because the US stopped selling the car in 1998, later model-year cars are mostly imports.
