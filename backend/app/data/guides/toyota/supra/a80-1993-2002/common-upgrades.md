# Power

The 2JZ-GTE bottom end handles substantial power, so most builds start with boost, fuel system, injectors, a stand-alone or piggyback ECU and an exhaust. Upgrade fuel pump and injectors before raising boost, and always tune on a dyno.

# Drivetrain

Higher power exposes the clutch and, on manual cars, the 6-speed gearbox. Plan a stronger clutch alongside power goals.

# Age-related maintenance

These cars are decades old. Refresh vacuum hoses, boost control lines, cooling system parts, bushings and sensors before modifying, and watch for rust, worn mounts and degraded wiring.
