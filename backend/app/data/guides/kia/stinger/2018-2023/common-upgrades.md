# Power

Tunes and bolt-ons for the 3.3 twin-turbo V6 usually combine a tune with an intake, downpipes or an exhaust. The 2.0T and 2.5T respond to a tune, intake and intercooler. A reputable tune matters more than hardware.

# Cooling and heat

Repeated hard driving can heat the intake air and the transmission. An upgraded intercooler and transmission cooler are sensible before chasing more power.

# Handling

Lowering springs or coilovers sharpen handling. Tires are the first upgrade, and the AWD cars benefit from fresh matched tires on all four corners.
