# Platform

The Stinger is a rear-wheel-drive-based liftback built on a shortened version of the Hyundai Genesis platform, engineered by former BMW M engineering head Albert Biermann. It was sold from 2017 to 2023 and has one transmission, an 8-speed automatic with paddles.

# Engines

Two engines were offered in the US. The 2.0 L turbo four makes 255 hp (252 in some markets). The 3.3 L twin-turbo V6 makes 365 hp and 376 lb-ft of torque from 1,300 to 4,500 rpm, and is the GT engine. The 2021 refresh added a 2.5 L turbo four (300 hp) and a variable exhaust on the V6. Parts for the 3.3TT, 2.0T and 2.5T engines are not interchangeable.

# Drivetrain and brakes

Cars were sold in RWD and AWD. The GT variants use Brembo brakes. The 2021 refresh changed the lights, infotainment and badge, so check model year for body parts.
