# Body styles and timing

The 10th generation Civic launched as a sedan in late 2015, followed by the coupe in early 2016 and the hatchback in 2017. Check which body style you own before choosing parts, since the hatchback only came with a turbo engine.

# Engines

Most trims use a naturally aspirated 2.0 L engine or a 1.5 L direct-injected turbo engine. The hatchback is turbo-only. The Type R is a separate case: a hatchback with a 2.0 L turbocharged i-VTEC engine. Turbo-specific mods such as intakes, intercoolers, downpipes and tunes only apply to the 1.5T and Type R engines, not the 2.0 L naturally aspirated cars.

# Performance trims

The Si (sedan or coupe) uses the 1.5 L turbo and comes only with a 6-speed manual. The Type R is also manual only. The Sport hatchback is available with a CVT or a 6-speed manual. From the 2019 model year a Sport trim was added to the sedan and coupe between LX and EX, with 18-inch wheels but the 2.0 L engine, so it is not a turbo car.

# Transmissions

Higher trims of the 1.5T and 2.0 L engines are CVT only, and Touring trims are CVT only. A manual swap or clutch upgrade question therefore only applies to manual-equipped cars such as the Si, Type R and manual Sport or EX variants.
